Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026. RektBull is run by {{OPERATOR}}. Questions or requests: {{CONTACT}}.

In short

  • Your account is your wallet address. We don't ask for your name, and we never see your keys or seed phrase.
  • We keep what each feature needs to work, and no more. No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, no tracking cookies, and we don't sell data.
  • The browser extension sends us the request your wallet is about to sign and the site asking, only when a site asks to sign, to check it. It doesn't send your browsing.
  • Wallet addresses, approvals and transactions are public on their blockchains. We read public data; nobody can delete what's onchain.
  • You can download everything we hold about your account, and delete your personal data, from the Safety page.

What the browser extension does

  • It runs on every site, because any site can ask your wallet to sign. It only reads a request when a site asks your wallet to sign something (a transaction, a signature, a sign-in), and does nothing on pages that don't.
  • For that request it sends RektBull: the request itself, your wallet address, the chain, and the address of the site asking (for example https://app.example, not the full page address). We use them to run the check, and keep only the kind of request and the chain.
  • It keeps your account link and settings in the extension's own storage on your computer.
  • It doesn't read your browsing history, page contents, passwords or form data, and it can't sign anything. Your wallet still shows every request.

The MetaMask Snap

The RektBull Snap sends RektBull the same things as the extension (the request being confirmed, your wallet address, the chain and the requesting site's origin) when MetaMask shows you a transaction or signature to confirm, and we keep the same (only the kind of request and the chain). Every 30 minutes it asks RektBull for new risk monitor alerts to show as MetaMask notifications. Its link to your account is kept in MetaMask's encrypted storage on your device; unlink it from the Extension page or remove the Snap in MetaMask.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in requests, and linking or email-confirmation codes: deleted a day after they expire.
  • Daily free-use counters (including hashed IP addresses): 40 days.
  • Monthly points and pass counters: 13 months.
  • Alert delivery records: 90 days. Risk monitor alerts: a year.
  • Revoked or expired extension links: 30 days.
  • Newsletter sign-ups never confirmed: 7 days. A confirmed address: until you unsubscribe, which deletes it at once. An address the email provider says doesn't take mail is deleted.
  • Hosting request logs: 30 days.
  • Everything else, such as your watched wallets, alert channels and settings: until you delete it or your data.
  • Payment and trade records (credits, top-ups, deposits, passes, paid checks, claims and yield moves and their fees, trades): kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, tied to your wallet address.

Your choices

  • Download your data: everything we hold about your account, as one file (Safety page → Your account → Your data).
  • Delete your data: removes your email address, alert channels, watched wallets and alerts, AI agent settings, extension links, trading settings and free-use counters. Payment and trade records stay, as above. Stop automatic trading first if it's on.
  • Stop the weekly digest any time with the unsubscribe link in any issue (or your mail app's unsubscribe button): your address is deleted at once.
  • Stop alerts any time: /stop in Telegram, the unsubscribe link in any email, your browser's notification settings, or Remove on the Safety page.
  • Unlink the extension from its popup or from the Extension page, or uninstall it.
  • For anything else, including correcting or objecting to what we hold, write to {{CONTACT}}.

Security

RektBull never holds your funds or your keys: your wallet signs everything. Our own signing keys live in a hardware security module (Google Cloud KMS). Traffic is encrypted, secrets are kept out of the code, and every check names where its facts come from.

Children

RektBull is for adults. Don't use it if you're under 18.
