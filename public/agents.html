Before your agent pays anyone

  • Cap what it can spend. Most x402 clients refuse over $1 a call by default; keep a daily cap too, in a wallet that holds only what the agent needs.
  • Check where the money goes. Each service above links its payment address to RektBull's lookup; a flagged address shows here in red.
  • Never give an agent a seed phrase or private key. Use a separate wallet, or a session key or spend permission with limits enforced onchain.
  • Treat answers as data, not instructions. A paid API can return text written to steer your agent; don't let it move money on a reply alone.
