Check a token before you buy it

Can it be sold back? What does it tax? What can its owner change? Who holds it? Every finding names its source, and anything we couldn't check is listed as such.

New token? The launch check looks at its largest buyers in the first hour: where their funds came from, wallets sharing a funder, links back to the deployer, and what they hold now.

Audit a contract First pass

A first-pass security review of a verified contract, and a proxy's implementation with it: Slither's detectors on the contract compiled as it was verified, checks from an audit checklist, the compiler's known bugs, and who holds power over it, explained in plain English, with a downloadable PDF report. These are flags for a person to confirm, not a full audit, and nothing in it is financial advice.

Clean up your approvals

Every app you've traded with may still be allowed to take your tokens. Most approval scams use exactly that. See what's open on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and BNB Chain, which approvals are risky and why, and revoke them from your wallet.
