What it costs

Checking is free.

  • The fee is a plain transfer of the claimed tokens to RektBull's fee wallet, which your wallet shows next to the claim. No approval is asked for, and RektBull never holds your tokens.
  • Wallets that can bundle calls send the claim and the fee together, so the fee is paid only if the claim works. Other wallets send the claim first.
  • It's a share of what the simulation shows arriving in your wallet, not of what the claim says.
  • No fee when the tokens can't be transferred yet, or when the fee would be worth less than the gas to send it.
  • Claiming on the project's own site is always free. Every result links to it.
