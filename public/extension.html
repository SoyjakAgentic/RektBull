Check before you sign

The RektBull extension catches what your wallet is about to sign (a transaction, a permit, an NFT listing, a sign-in) and checks it first: it simulates what would leave your wallet, rates every spender and recipient, and checks the site against phishing lists. A risky request stops with a warning that says why, and where the facts come from; a safe one goes straight to your wallet.

  • Works with any browser wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Phantom, Rabby…) on any site.
  • Sends RektBull only the request being signed and the site's address, nothing else from your browsing.
  • Never touches your keys and can't sign anything: your wallet still shows every request.
  • 1 free point per check (30 a month), or every check with Safety Pro.
