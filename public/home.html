What RektBull does

SafetyCheck a token before you buy it, clean up approvals, and get alerts when something changes in your wallet. Find moneyAirdrops and rewards you haven't claimed, and where your stablecoins and ETH could earn more. GovernanceThe DAO votes your wallet can cast, on Snapshot and onchain, each proposal explained from its own text. Pre-sign checkIn your browser or MetaMask: what a transaction or signature would do, before you sign it.

What it costs

  • Checking is free: this checkup, the approval preview, the airdrop and rewards check, and DAO votes.

You pay only for work that's delivered: a run that fails is given back. Pay with USDT or USDC (or many other coins through NOWPayments); bots pay per call with x402. Nothing renews automatically, and RektBull never asks your wallet for a spending approval to bill you.

How it stays on your side

  • Your wallet signs everything. RektBull prepares transactions and shows what each one does; it never holds your funds or keys.
  • Every finding names its source, and a check that couldn't run says why. Nothing found means nothing in those sources, not nothing anywhere.
  • No recommendations on how to vote, and AI summaries only explain the text they're given.

For bots and AI agents

POST /v1/lookup with {"query"} (an address) says what it is on each chain; POST /v1/checkup with {"wallet"} runs the free checks of every desk. Both are free, with a daily limit. Paid runs take x402 (USDC on Base); prices at /v1/catalog.
