Does RektBull ever hold my funds or keys?

No. RektBull prepares transactions and your wallet signs them. It never asks for a seed phrase or a private key, and paying for RektBull never needs a token approval.

Can RektBull trade with my money?

Only if you give it a limited permission from your wallet: a most-per-trade and most-per-day that your wallet writes into the Trade Router, for up to 30 days. Trades can only go back into your wallet, never anywhere else, and you can revoke the permission at any time. Without it, you sign every trade yourself.

Is the checkup really free?

Yes, with a daily limit: 3 a day without signing in, 10 when you sign in with your wallet. Signing in is free and moves nothing.

Why is the claim fee a share of the tokens?

New airdrops often have no price yet. The fee is a share of what arrives, in a transfer you sign with the claim, so you only pay if the claim works. Claiming on the project's own site is always free.

Which chains does it read?

Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and BNB Chain for wallets, and Solana for token checks. A chain that couldn't be read is named, never treated as empty.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's an automated check of known patterns. It never recommends a vote or a trade, and a clean result is not proof that something is safe.

What happens if a check fails?

You aren't charged. Credits and free points come back, and x402 payments settle only after a run succeeds.
