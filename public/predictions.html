Prediction markets on Polymarket, from your own wallet

Browse Polymarket's markets and trade them from your own Polymarket wallet. Your wallet signs every order and every set-up step; your signed orders go from this browser straight to Polymarket. RektBull never holds your money, your keys or your Polymarket API credentials.

Spot tokens are on the Trade desk · perps on the Perps page.

Not financial advice. A prediction market can lose everything you put in, and RektBull never picks an outcome. Figures are read from Polymarket's own APIs, each named where it's shown.
