Trade desk

Trade with limits you set

Every token is checked before you buy. Your wallet signs each trade, or gives RektBull a limited permission that can only trade inside limits your wallet writes onchain, and that you can revoke at any time. RektBull can never withdraw.

Perpetuals on Hyperliquid: the Perps page trades from your own Hyperliquid account, with a trading key that can never withdraw. Prediction markets on Polymarket trade from your own wallet.

New tokens and meme coins: the Launches desk shows the newest, with their warning signs, and checks a young token for a bundled launch before you buy.

Not financial advice. The trade desk checks each token and acts only within limits you set and sign. It never picks what to buy or sell, and trading can lose money.

1. Connect your wallet

Your funds stay in your wallet. RektBull checks each trade and builds it; your wallet shows it and you sign it.

2. Trade

RektBull checks the trade as soon as you pick a token, so a click goes straight to your wallet. Every check runs again on a fresh price when you click.

Speed

How fast your trades get into a block, set under Safety settings below. Standard leaves the fee to your wallet; Fast and Fastest offer the fee shown, read from the chain's last 20 blocks, and your wallet shows it before you sign. They send each step of a trade on its own, with that fee.

Orders on Solana limit buy · stop-loss · take-profit · Meme Watch exit

Your wallet places the order and approves only what it trades: the coins it sells, or for a limit buy, the SOL it spends (wrapped, in your own wallet). RektBull's order program then lets RektBull trade them for you when the order fires: at your price or better, before its end date, with what they bring going only to your wallet. Nothing else of yours can be touched, and nobody holds your coins or your SOL. RektBull's fee is 1% of the SOL a fill brings or spends; nothing if it never fills.

An order is waiting on a one-time setup from your wallet: pump.fun's volume accounts for your order address (about 0.0037 SOL, once).

Each step sells its part of the coins (of the share above) when their value is up by its own amount.

Orders on BNB Chain and Base limit buy · stop-loss · take-profit · Meme Watch exit

Your wallet signs the order (a signature, nothing is sent) and approves RektBull's Meme Router for only what your orders trade: the coins they sell, or for a limit buy, the WETH or WBNB they spend, in your own wallet. The router then lets RektBull trade them for you when the order fires: at your price or better, before its end date, with what they bring going only to your wallet, in the same transaction. Nothing else of yours can be touched, and nobody holds your coins. RektBull's fee is 1% of what a fill brings or spends, and RektBull pays the network fee; nothing if it never fills.

Each step sells its part of the coins (of the share above) when their value is up by its own amount.
