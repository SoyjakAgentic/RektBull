Trade desk

Trade with limits you set

Every token is checked before you buy. Your wallet signs each trade, or gives RektBull a limited permission that can only trade inside limits your wallet writes onchain, and that you can revoke at any time. RektBull can never withdraw.

New tokens and meme coins: the Launches desk shows the newest, with their warning signs, and checks a young token for a bundled launch before you buy.

Not financial advice. The trade desk checks each token and acts only within limits you set and sign. It never picks what to buy or sell, and trading can lose money.

1. Connect your wallet

Your funds stay in your wallet. RektBull checks each trade and builds it; your wallet shows it and you sign it.

2. Trade

RektBull checks the trade as soon as you pick a token, so a click goes straight to your wallet. Every check runs again on a fresh price when you click.
