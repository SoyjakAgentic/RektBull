RektBull trades for you within limits your own wallet writes into the RektBull Trade Router. It can never change them and can never withdraw: trades only go back into your wallet, and the fee can't exceed 1%. The router isn't audited yet, so this runs on the test network only, with test tokens. Only tokens the router has a reference price for can be traded automatically.
