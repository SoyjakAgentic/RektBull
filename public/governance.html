On-chain governors

RektBull reads Uniswap, Compound, ENS, Gitcoin, Hop and Arbitrum's governors for every wallet. Add another OpenZeppelin Governor or Governor Bravo by its address.

Your wallet

Connect the wallet you're voting with, to sign. A vote is a signature, not a transaction: it costs no gas and moves nothing. Sign in (free) for more checks a day and for summaries with your points; Safety Pro adds alerts when a vote opens or is about to close.

For bots and AI agents

POST /v1/governance/check with {"wallet"} (free); POST /v1/governance/summary with {"proposal_id"} (1 point, $0.10 by x402, or Safety Pro); POST /v1/governance/votes/prepare with {"wallet", "proposal_id", "choice", "reason"} returns EIP-712 typed data to sign; POST /v1/governance/votes with {"message", "sig"} sends it to Snapshot.
