Tokens launched on four.meme that haven't graduated to PancakeSwap yet: they're bought and sold on four.meme's own contract, with its 1% fee each way. Pay with BNB (99% buys on the curve; RektBull's 1% is a separate transfer sent with the buy), or with USDT or USDC, swapped into BNB through 0x with the 1% taken there. A sale's 1% is paid by four.meme's contract from the sale itself, in the same transaction. Your wallet signs each step.
