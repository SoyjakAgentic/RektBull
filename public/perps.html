2. A trading key for this browser

Hyperliquid signs orders for its own chain (chain id 1337), which wallets such as MetaMask won't sign. So, as Hyperliquid's own app does, this page makes a trading key in your browser and your wallet approves it once. Hyperliquid lets that key place and cancel orders and set leverage. It can't withdraw or transfer.

  • The key is made and kept in this browser only. It is never sent to RektBull or anyone else; orders are signed here and only the signature leaves.
  • It expires by itself after 30 days. Clearing this site's data removes it, and you can end it at any time below.
  • Anyone who can use this browser profile can trade with it until then, so don't approve one on a shared computer.
