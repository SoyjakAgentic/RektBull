# Positioning

*Decided 4 November 2026. The site's copy, the SEO plan ([SEO.md](SEO.md)) and
the launch campaign ([LAUNCH_CAMPAIGN.md](LAUNCH_CAMPAIGN.md)) follow this. If
it changes, change those with it.*

## In one line

> **RektBull: don't get rekt.** It checks what you're about to sign, hold,
> claim or pay for, and tells you where every fact comes from. For you, and
> for your AI agents.

## The category: crypto safety

RektBull sells one thing, **safety**, to two audiences: **people** with wallets,
and the **AI agents** that act for them.

| Label | Why not lead with it |
|---|---|
| "Blockchain intel" | Puts RektBull next to Arkham, Nansen and Chainalysis: paid data, enterprise buyers, and "who owns this address", which RektBull doesn't do. It also suggests surveillance, against the privacy promise. **Intel is the content we publish, not what we sell.** |
| "Blockchain security" | Searches for it want smart-contract auditors (CertiK, OpenZeppelin, Trail of Bits). Say **crypto safety** or **wallet safety**: what we do, and what people type. |
| "Blockchain agents" | Crowded and hype-led, and it hides the real angle: **the check an agent runs before it signs or pays**, which almost no one offers. |
| All three as equals | Reads as "does everything", which tells no one why to come. |

## Who it's for

1. **People with a wallet** who have been scared, or burned, by a drainer, a bad
   approval or a scam token. They want to know "am I exposed?" without reading
   contracts. *This is the first audience; most traffic and trust come from them.*
2. **Builders of AI agents** that hold or move money (on Base and with x402
   especially). They need checks their agent can call, and a way to pay per
   call. *Second audience: fewer people, earlier market, and a channel where
   few others are.*

## What we say, and what proves it

| Promise | Proof (shipped) |
|---|---|
| **Check before it costs you** | Free wallet checkup (approvals, claims, idle money, votes on six chains); token checks; the pre-sign extension; the risk monitor |
| **Every fact has a source** | Each finding names it (GoPlus, Etherscan, DefiLlama, Merkl, onchain); a check that couldn't run says why; AI writes only from sourced facts |
| **Your wallet signs everything** | RektBull never holds funds or keys; every revoke, claim, trade or vote is prepared for the user's wallet; automatic trading only within limits written onchain |
| **For your agents too** | The MCP server (18 read-only tools), x402 pay-per-call, and the x402 services ranked by real payers with their warning signs |
| **Money, with the record shown** | Rewards to claim, campaigns and rates, each with the protocol's hack and audit record. This comes *after* safety, never in its place |

## The order

1. **Hook (free):** the wallet checkup. Paste an address, see what matters in
   seconds.
2. **Keep (habit):** the extension before every signature, monitor alerts, and
   the weekly digest by email.
3. **Earn:** Safety Pro, claim fees (a share of what's claimed), and x402 runs.

Opportunities, yield and trading sit **behind** safety: a safety brand earns the
right to show where money is; a money brand never earns the trust a safety
brand needs.

## Words

**Use:** check, flag, warn, show the record, at risk, sourced, your wallet
signs, don't get rekt.

**Don't use:**
- **"Safe", "secure", "protected", "guaranteed".** RektBull never calls a token,
  dapp or campaign safe, and the marketing can't either. Say "checked",
  "flagged", "no hack recorded", "here's its record". It's honest, and it
  limits liability when something slips through.
- **"Earn X%", "best yield", "high returns".** Advertising returns is a
  regulated financial promotion in the UK and EU (the same rule as the
  newsletter's sponsored posts). Show rates as facts with risks, never as a
  pitch.
- **"Intel", "track any wallet", "who owns".** Not what we do, and not who we
  are.
- **"AI-powered".** Say what the AI does, and that a person reviews what's
  published.

## What sets RektBull apart

Pre-sign warnings are becoming standard inside wallets (MetaMask shows security
alerts itself), so "we warn before you sign" alone isn't a difference. The
differences are:

- **The whole wallet, not one moment:** approvals, claims, idle money, votes,
  holdings and alerts, not only the next signature.
- **Sources on everything.** Most checkers give a score; RektBull says where
  each fact came from.
- **Agents as first-class users:** an MCP server and x402 checks, which wallet
  security tools don't offer.
- **No custody, ever,** and nothing to subscribe to: pay per use in crypto, or
  a pass that doesn't renew by itself.

## Where the copy stands

Checked on 4 November 2026: the site already follows this. The hero leads with
"Don't get rekt" and the checks; the FAQ says a clean result isn't proof of
safety; no page calls anything safe. The hero now also says the same checks
work for your AI agent.

**Open question for the owner:** the logo's shield has an inner mark that's
currently invisible (its colour token, `--accent-ink`, was never defined). Made
visible, it reads as a **¥ sign**, which suits a safety brand badly. The site,
the icons and the preview image use the plain shield people see today. A real
mark (a bull, horns, a check) is a design decision for later.
